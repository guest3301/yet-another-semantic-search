Admissions at Greenfield University

We welcome applications from qualified students who are passionate about learning and committed to making a difference. Our admission process is designed to identify candidates with strong academic potential and a drive for personal growth.

Undergraduate Admissions

For all undergraduate programs, candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks (45% for reserved categories). Admission is based on merit in the qualifying examination, followed by a personal interview for shortlisted candidates.

  • Application Deadline: 31 March 2026 for the July intake.
  • Application Fee: ₹500 (non‑refundable).
  • Documents required: Mark sheets, transfer certificate, character certificate, and passport‑size photographs.

Postgraduate Admissions

For Master's and Doctoral programs, candidates need a relevant undergraduate degree with at least 55% marks. A valid score in the national entrance test (e.g., GATE, CAT, NET) is mandatory for most programs.

  • Application Deadline: 15 May 2026 for the August session.
  • Selection Process: Entrance test score, academic record, and a research proposal (for PhD applicants).

International Students

We have a dedicated International Student Office that assists with visa processing, accommodation, and cultural integration. International applicants need a valid IELTS/TOEFL score (if native language is not English).
