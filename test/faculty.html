Our Distinguished Faculty

Our faculty members are leaders in their fields, combining deep research expertise with a passion for teaching. They are committed to mentoring students and advancing knowledge.

Dr. Anand Sharma Professor, Computer Science & Engineering. Ph.D. (IIT Bombay), 25+ years of experience. Research areas: AI, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing.
Dr. Priya Mehta Professor, Physics. Ph.D. (University of Cambridge). Specialises in condensed matter physics and quantum materials. Published over 100 papers in top journals.
Prof. Ravi Verma Associate Professor, Business Administration. MBA (Harvard), former CFO of a Fortune 500 company. Teaches corporate finance and strategic management.
Dr. Sneha Reddy Assistant Professor, Environmental Science. Ph.D. (UC Berkeley). Research on climate change adaptation and sustainable urban planning.
Dr. Kiran Patel Professor, English Literature. Ph.D. (Oxford). Expert in postcolonial literature and creative writing. Author of 5 books.

We also have visiting faculty from industry and international universities who bring practical insights to the classroom.
