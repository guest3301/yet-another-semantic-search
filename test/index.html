Welcome to Greenfield University

Greenfield University is a premier institution of higher learning dedicated to shaping the leaders of tomorrow. Founded in 1985, we have consistently ranked among the top 10 universities in the region for excellence in research and teaching.

Our sprawling campus spans 200 acres of lush greenery, housing state‑of‑the‑art laboratories, a central library with over 500,000 volumes, and modern hostels for 2,000+ students.

We offer 50+ undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across six schools: Engineering, Sciences, Business, Humanities, Health Sciences, and Education.
