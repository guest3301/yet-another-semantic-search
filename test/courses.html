Our Academic Programs

Greenfield University offers a diverse range of programs across disciplines. Our curriculum is constantly updated to meet industry and societal needs.

Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech)

Specializations: Computer Science & Engineering, Electronics & Communication, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering.

Duration: 4 years (8 semesters).

Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.)

Majors: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Environmental Science.

Duration: 3 years (6 semesters).

Eligibility: 10+2 with Science stream.

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Specializations: Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, Operations, Entrepreneurship.

Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).

Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks.

Master of Arts (M.A.) in English

Focus: Literary theory, world literature, creative writing, and language pedagogy.

Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)

Fields: Engineering, Sciences, Management, Humanities, Social Sciences.

Duration: Minimum 3 years (full-time).

Eligibility: Master's degree with at least 55% marks and a research proposal.
