About Greenfield University

Greenfield University was established in 1985 with the vision to provide world‑class education that integrates academic excellence with ethical leadership. Our mission is to empower students with knowledge, skills, and character to contribute positively to society.

We are a non‑profit institution recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited with an 'A++' grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC).

Our Core Values

  • Integrity – We uphold honesty and transparency in all our actions.
  • Excellence – We strive for the highest standards in teaching, research, and service.
  • Inclusivity – We embrace diversity and ensure equal opportunities for all.
  • Sustainability – We are committed to environmental stewardship and sustainable practices.

Campus Facilities

Our campus is a hub of activity with:

  • A central library open 24/7 with digital access to 50,000+ journals.
  • Modern laboratories equipped with the latest technology for research.
  • Sports complex with indoor and outdoor facilities including a swimming pool.
  • Student activity centre with clubs for arts, music, drama, and entrepreneurship.
  • On‑campus health centre and counselling services.

We take pride in our community and encourage every student to participate in extracurricular activities that complement their academic journey.
